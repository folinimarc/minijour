Where your journal lives

On your device
Writing saves automatically in your browser. Once installed, the app works offline. No minijour account, analytics, or ads.
In your cloud storage, if you choose
Connect your own account to write across devices. Text, editing history, photos, and filenames are encrypted before upload. Your cloud password stays with you.
In a file you can keep
Download your journal as Markdown, with photos and editing history in a ZIP. You can restore it here or read the Markdown elsewhere.

The local journal and downloaded ZIPs are unencrypted. Your cloud provider can see file sizes and upload times. Keep your cloud password safe: there is no password reset. Without cloud sync or a downloaded backup, clearing browser data removes your journal.
